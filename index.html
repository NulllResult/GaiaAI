What GAIA is

GAIA is a computational system that reads raw genomes, maps the variants it finds, and asks one question: what is this sequence actually doing? It does not wait for a hypothesis. It derives one from the data.

The result is a protocol — a specific set of compounds, at specific doses, for a specific person. Not a list of targets. A treatment plan.

How it learns

Three loops, running continuously. Each one improves the system without anyone telling it what to look for.

Loop 01

Reading raw sequence

Every region is read for what it can hold. The system finds the sites a natural compound can reach on its own — and the ones that require a covalent bond to stay anchored.

  • Scan the genome for what can be targeted
  • Read each site — reversible or covalent
  • Mark what a natural compound can hold on its own
  • Flag what only a refined molecule could reach
Loop 02

Combining what fits

Natural compounds are mapped to what they can reach. When two or more hit the same pathway from different angles, they act together — molecular synergy, not single-agent suppression.

  • Match each natural compound to the sites it reaches
  • Combine compounds that converge on the same pathway
  • Reserve covalent binding for what must stay anchored
  • Refine a molecule only when nature falls short
Loop 03

Delivering the plan

A protocol is only as good as its delivery. GAIA chooses the form — pill, capsule, liquid, injection — and builds the full plan around it: what to take, when, and for how long.

  • Choose the form — oral, capsule, liquid, injection
  • Formulate for stability, absorption, and shelf life
  • Set dose, timing, and escalation rules
  • Deliver the plan — what, when, and how

The math underneath

Sequence is raw potential. Meaning emerges only when a pattern is applied in a context. We wrote the math that reads both — not assembled from other people's tools, but derived from the structure of the genome itself.

Boundaries are where biology shifts

The genome isn't uniform. Composition drifts, structure loosens, conservation fades. A boundary is placed wherever those shifts align — and the same logic runs at three scales at once.

Every segment earns a signature

Between boundaries, each region is described not by a label but by a fingerprint — its composition, its reading frame, its skew, its conservation across deep time. The classifier is not told what to look for. It discovers which combinations of features separate one kind of region from another, and holds that separation across chromosomes and species.

No threshold is chosen. The boundaries, the features, and the decision surface are all discovered from labeled sequence — and every equation is written from scratch.

Physics and machine learning — together

Two methods. Each one covers what the other misses.

Physics can score anything — cannot learn

Every candidate gets a physical score. Lennard-Jones for contacts, Coulomb for electrostatics, solvation for how the pocket reshapes, entropy for what the ligand loses when it binds. The number is real and the physics is inspectable — but the equations are fixed.

Physics cannot get better at what it does. It does not learn from the compounds it gets wrong.

Machine learning can learn anything — cannot explain it

The same complex is read as a feature vector. The classifier discovers which combinations of features separate binders from non-binders — patterns no one hand-coded, thresholds no one set. Trained on labeled data, it finds what physics alone would miss.

ML cannot justify its decisions. When it disagrees with physics, nobody can say which one is right.

Agreement — physics confirms the classifier's confidence Divergence — flagged, not promoted

When the two methods agree, the score is trusted. When they diverge, the compound is flagged — because a disagreement between a physical calculation and a learned judgment is not noise. It is information.

Evidence across every layer

Predictions are checked against three independent kinds of evidence — computational, cellular, physiological. A claim that survives all three is worth acting on.

In silico

Computational

Every prediction is stress-tested in pure computation before anything touches a cell. Confidence is estimated from the spread of agreement across independent methods.

  • Structure prediction — modeled protein geometry
  • Binding simulation — strength across methods
  • Cross-validation — held-out data
  • Confidence intervals — agreement across approaches
In vitro

Cellular

Predictions are compared against real cell-based assays from public literature. Pathway activity, gene expression, and cytotoxicity all feed back into the model.

  • Cell-line assays — published screens
  • Pathway response — signaling under treatment
  • Gene expression — transcript-level confirmation
  • Cytotoxicity profiles — safe dose ranges
In vivo

Physiological

Protocols are evaluated against animal model studies and human trial data. Where the model predicts an outcome, that outcome is checked against published physiological responses.

  • Animal disease models — established systems
  • Clinical trial data — dosing, outcomes, events
  • Human cohorts — time-to-response
  • Longitudinal outcomes — durability at follow-up

Most tools solve one piece.
GAIA runs the whole chain — and then goes further.

It reads the genome backward.

Standard pipelines pick a target, then ask which variants matter. GAIA starts from the raw genome and the patient's own symptom pattern, and works back to the genes actually producing the disease. Nothing is hand-coded. Everything is derived.

It reawakens what disease silences.

Disease doesn't just damage tissue — it shuts down the repair pathways that would heal it. GAIA identifies compounds that reactivate those silenced signals, the difference between managing a disease and reversing it.

It reverses, not just manages.

The pathways that repair tissue are the same ones that repair DNA. When GAIA reawakens them, it does not stop at the joint — it reverses up to ten years of accumulated DNA-repair deficit in simulation. Reversal, not management.

It checks itself before it speaks.

A claim that survives only computation is not a claim. It is a guess. Verification is not a phase — it runs alongside every prediction, and the results are published.

It has already run once.

End to end, on Ankylosing Spondylitis. In simulation the full pipeline — genome read, variants mapped, compounds ranked, protocol designed — took BASDAI from 6.9 to 1.0 over 12 months. Clinical remission. The result, the numbers, and the method are public.
